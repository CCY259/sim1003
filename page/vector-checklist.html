Chapter 11 Checklist

  1. Find a vector given two points.
  2. Determine the angle between two vectors.
  3. Find the unit vector in the direction of a given vector.
  4. Explain the relationship between the scalar component and the vector projection.
  5. State the characterization of orthogonal vectors.
  6. Find a vector that is perpendicular to two given vectors, and explain what would happen if only one vector is given.
  7. Calculate the area of a triangle and a parallelogram determined by vectors.
  8. Calculate the area of a triangle given three points and a parallelogram given four points. Discuss the method on parallelogram.
  9. Calculate the volume of a parallelepiped determined by vectors. Do the same if only the points are given.
  10. List the components used in describing lines and planes.
  11. State the characterization of parallel lines.
  12. State the characterization of coplanar lines.
  13. State the characterization of three coplanar vectors.
  14. State the characterization of parallel planes.
  15. State the characterization of perpendicular planes.
  16. Find the equation of a plane containing three given points.
  17. Find the equation of a plane containing two given lines.
  18. Find a vector perpendicular to a given plane.
  19. Find the point of intersection of two lines.
  20. Find the point of intersection between a line and a plane.
  21. Find the line of intersection of two planes.
  22. Calculate the distance between
    1. a point and a line;
    2. a point and a plane;
    3. two planes;
    4. a line and a plane;
    5. two lines.
  23. Calculate the angle between
    1. two lines;
    2. two planes;
    3. a line and a plane.
